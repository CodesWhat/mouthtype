Mouthtype 0.1.0-alpha.6

Choose and remember a microphone in Settings. A missing chosen microphone is reported instead of silently switching inputs.
Optionally quiet supported output audio while recording, then restore the state Mouthtype owns. This is off by default.
Optionally show provisional words above the recording pill. The completed take is still transcribed and inserted once. This is off by default.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later. This is an alpha; review text before sending.
Microphone switching, supported output hardware and provisional display still need coordinated physical acceptance. Fresh installation and physical update installation remain unverified.
The Linux source candidate is experimental and separately tested. Arch userland or headless Sway component checks do not certify a physical Arch/Omarchy desktop.
