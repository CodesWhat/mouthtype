Mouthtype 0.1.0-alpha.4

New Settings window with General, Writing, Appearance and Diagnostics.
Choose Hold to talk or Press to toggle for your dictation shortcut.
Set a cleanup tone for individual apps while keeping the global default.
A quieter menu keeps daily actions handy and moves diagnostics into Settings.
Old interrupted recordings no longer automatically reappear after newer completed takes.
Check for signed updates in the app and choose whether to enable automatic update checks.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later. This is an alpha; review text before sending.
Broader app delivery, fresh installs and physical update installation still need tester feedback.
