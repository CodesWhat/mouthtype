Mouthtype 0.1.0-alpha.7

Transcribe one WAV, AIFF or M4A file from the menu. Files can be up to 10 minutes and 512 MB. Review the full raw result, then explicitly Copy or Save Text. Cancel waits for the current recognition operation to settle. Your original audio stays unchanged; nothing is pasted automatically or added to dictation statistics.

Includes the alpha.6 microphone selector, optional Quiet other audio and optional Live words. Mouthtype remembers a chosen microphone and reports a missing device instead of silently switching. Quieting and provisional words are off by default. Preview words may change; the completed take is still transcribed and inserted once.

The source archive includes experimental Linux speech preparation and a daemon/CLI candidate targeting Arch/Omarchy, Hyprland and PipeWire. This is source-only, without a Linux GUI or packaged desktop release. Model preparation is explicit; recovery-only delivery is the default. Physical Arch/Omarchy acceptance remains open.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later. This is an alpha; review text before sending.
Microphone switching, supported output hardware, provisional display and real file-recognition acceptance still need coordinated testing. Fresh installation and physical update installation remain unverified.
