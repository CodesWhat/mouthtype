Cold prepare is the first process for the engine: download plus load, with CoreML compile folded into load. Warm load is `prepare()` in a fresh process with the models already on disk. First transcribe is the first untimed transcription in the warm process, which absorbs any lazy CoreML specialization that `prepare()` did not. Short-clip median is the median of per-clip medians for clips of 10 s or less. Overall RTFx is total audio seconds over the summed per-clip median processing seconds. WER is corpus-level (total edits over total reference words) on the last timed run of each clip. Peak RSS is the warm process after loading and transcribing the whole corpus.

Engines marked `(models already on disk)` in the cold column had their files cached before this run, so their cold number is not a first-ever download.
