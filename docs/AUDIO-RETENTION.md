# Saved audio

In **Settings → General → Saved audio**, choose how long Mouthtype keeps completed recordings: **14 days**, **30 days**, **90 days**, or **No age limit**. Choose a completed-audio size limit of **2 GB** or **10 GB**. The defaults remain **90 days and 2 GB**.

Changes apply to existing completed recordings the next time Mouthtype launches. Startup maintenance removes expired completed WAVs, then the oldest remaining completed WAVs until they fit the selected size limit. No age limit still has a size limit. A change doesn't delete recordings immediately or interrupt a recording in progress.

These controls only affect audio generated by Mouthtype. Dictation text, word timings, saved spellings, phrases and surrounding-text behavior stay unchanged. Audio imported with **Transcribe Audio File…** stays at its original location and isn't part of this archive.

Unfinished recordings, crash-recovery partials and failed takes available for recovery are exempt from these completed-audio limits. Recovery audio can therefore use additional storage. It remains available through **Saved Recordings**; the existing Recover and Discard behavior is unchanged. Discard ends the recovery offer and doesn't promise to delete its audio immediately.

The next launch applies the selected policy to existing completed recordings. Retention settings don't restore audio previously removed. New archive metadata records the selected expiration date, or no expiration date when there is no age limit. When startup maintenance removes a completed recording, its audio location and expiration are cleared from its database row; its text and statistics remain.
