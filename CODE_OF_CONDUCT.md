# Code of Conduct

Mouthtype follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/) in full. It applies in issues, pull requests, Discussions and any other space where people represent the project.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep feedback about the work, and don't harass anyone. Maintainers can remove, edit or reject comments, commits and contributions that don't fit, and can ban people who keep breaking these rules.

## Reporting

Email [security@codeswhat.com](mailto:security@codeswhat.com) with "Mouthtype conduct" in the subject. Reports are handled privately and the reporter's identity isn't shared without their consent. Enforcement follows the [Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) in the Covenant.
