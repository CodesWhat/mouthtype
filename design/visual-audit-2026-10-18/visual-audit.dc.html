02 / DISPLAY SCALE · “WILL IT BE GIANT OR TINY ON OTHER MACS?”

Same points everywhere. Different proportions.

macOS lays everything out in points, not pixels. The glyph is 16×15 pt, the Classic pill 28 pt tall, the Hi-Fi pill 146×32 pt (Standard) or 292×64 pt (Large 2×) on every Mac. A Retina display just spends 2 device pixels per point, which is why the @2x art is the same drawing doubled. What does change is how much of the screen those points occupy.

Three common screens at 1:4, pills drawn to scale at the bottom-centre placement (12 pt above the Dock line)
13" MACBOOK AIR · 1470×956 PT Hi-Fi Standard spans 9.9% of the width
Large 2× spans 19.9%
24" 1× MONITOR · 1920×1080 PT 7.6% / 15.2%. One art pixel = one device pixel,
so pixel art is at its crispest here
27" STUDIO DISPLAY · 2560×1440 PT 5.7% / 11.4%. Here Standard reads small from
arm’s length, and Large is the comfortable one
WHAT IS ALREADY RIGHT

Point-based sizes, template glyphs, 1× and 2× art, Classic bars snapped to even device pixels, and the Hi-Fi Large 2× option. Nothing will render at a surprising physical size on another Mac.

THE GAP

The size dial exists only for Hi-Fi, and only as a manual setting. Classic has no Large. The pill sits at the bottom edge, which is far from the eye on a 27" display and near the Dock on a laptop.

PROPOSAL (SEE 1J)

Pill size: Automatic / Standard / Large for both skins. Automatic picks Large when the pill’s screen is 2000 pt or wider. Pixel art stays integer-scaled, so 1× and 2× are the only steps.

A solid capsule on a glass operating system.

Locked: all three ship as pill styles alongside Classic and Hi-Fi (Glass, Matrix, Minimal), chosen in Appearance → Pill style.
